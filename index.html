The French Revolution, Napoleon and the Congress of Vienna

Study guide and practice tests built from Lectures 1 and 2, the Napoleon lecture, the Reign of Terror play (Jacques Dubois), and the Congress of Vienna reading.

Heads-up on dates: Lecture 1 says Louis XVI accepted the constitution and formed the Legislative Assembly by Sept 1789, while the Lecture 2 review says the Legislative Assembly formed in 1791. The guide and tests use 1791 for the Legislative Assembly. The original practice-test bank is retained; several other course-note discrepancies are flagged in the study notes.

Choose a round

Each round has 25 random questions. Questions and wrong answers are generated fresh from the fact bank every time ( facts), so every round is different. Four rounds cover about 100 questions.
