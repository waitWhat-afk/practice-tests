The French Revolution, Napoleon and the Congress of Vienna

Study guide and practice tests built from Lectures 1 and 2, the Napoleon lecture, the Reign of Terror play (Jacques Dubois), and the Congress of Vienna reading.

Heads-up on dates: Lecture 1 says Louis XVI accepted the constitution and formed the Legislative Assembly by Sept 1789, while the Lecture 2 review says the Legislative Assembly formed in 1791. The guide and tests use 1791 for the Legislative Assembly. Practice questions follow the course fact bank; several course-note discrepancies are flagged in the study notes.

Choose a round

Each round draws up to 25 questions from a -question bank. Every question has four closely related choices, with distractors written specifically for that question. Questions and option order are shuffled each round; rounds may overlap. The only date question is the storming of the Bastille.
