The French Revolution, Napoleon and the Congress of Vienna

Study guide and practice tests built from Lectures 1 and 2, the Napoleon lecture, the Reign of Terror play (Jacques Dubois), and the Congress of Vienna reading.

Heads-up on dates: Lecture 1 says Louis XVI accepted the constitution and formed the Legislative Assembly by Sept 1789, while the Lecture 2 review says the Legislative Assembly formed in 1791. The guide and tests use 1791 for the Legislative Assembly.

Key vocabulary: Congress of Vienna reading

Define in your own words: Congress of Vienna, Klemens von Metternich, Balance of Power, Legitimacy, Holy Alliance, Concert of Europe. Then answer the reading's two questions: (1) Which of Metternich's goals was most influential, and why? (2) Were the Vienna peace agreements reasonable and effective, and why? Also practice the Napoleon's Coronation questions 1 to 4 and the play's discussion questions on how the Terror's procedures were unfair, how the law made the Terror possible, who bears the most responsibility, and which Fifth Amendment rights were violated.

Choose a round

Each round has 25 random questions. Questions and wrong answers are generated fresh from the fact bank every time ( facts), so every round is different. Four rounds cover about 100 questions.
